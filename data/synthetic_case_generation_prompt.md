# Synthetic case-generation specification

Create ten fictional small- or medium-sized company scenarios for evaluating an AI pilot-planning assistant. Vary the industry, use case, pilot group size, duration, pain point, and operational constraint. Use only low-risk assistance tasks such as drafting, summarising, classifying public text, or preparing internal checklists. Do not include real organisations, personal data, confidential information, or high-stakes health, credit, hiring, legal, or security decisions.

For each case, provide:

- a case ID;
- company type and approximate size;
- one AI-assisted use case;
- a named participant role and group size;
- a pilot duration of two or three weeks;
- one current pain point;
- one privacy or operational constraint; and
- the same fixed pilot start date.

The resulting ten cases were reviewed for variety and frozen in `evaluation_cases.csv` before model outputs were compared.
